The checked-in wrapper selects Gradle 9.6.1; build and Android dependencies resolve from pinned Gradle files and Cargo.lock. The AnyWork mobile and mobile-system code is AGPL-3.0-only as declared by the workspace. AndroidX/Material, Kotlin and Logto are resolved dependencies; their source/license locations and versions remain in Gradle/Cargo metadata. The separate auth plugin owner maintains its SDK-specific provenance.
