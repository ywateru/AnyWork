# CyberBus and EasyTier LGPL compliance

AnyWork links the CyberBus transport statically in its native applications and
embeds the EasyTier core in its browser overlay. This file records the
distribution obligations; it is not a substitute for the license texts.

## Corresponding source

Every release must include `artifacts/corresponding-source.tar.gz` and
`artifacts/corresponding-source.tar.gz.sha256`, generated by
`scripts/corresponding-source.py`. The archive contains the exact tracked
CyberBus and EasyTier revisions, including CyberBus `packaging/NOTICE.md`,
`packaging/RELINK.md`, license files, vendored EasyTier and `Cargo.lock`.
The AnyWork application source and its build scripts are in
`artifacts/anywork-source.tar.gz`.

The public static site exposes the same files at `/corresponding-source` and
`/source`. Keep both offers available for at least the period required by the
applicable licenses, and do not replace them with a link that can disappear.

## Rebuilding and relinking

Native users can modify `cyberbus-transport` or vendored EasyTier and rebuild
with the locked Cargo source. Follow the upstream `packaging/RELINK.md`
instructions for the relevant target and preserve the public JNI/C/Apple
symbols when replacing a library. The browser runtime can be rebuilt with
`scripts/build-easytier-browser.sh` from the EasyTier checkout recorded in
`web/public/easytier-browser.PROVENANCE.json`.

AnyWork's native binaries are statically linked. Distributors must not impose
terms that prohibit reverse engineering for debugging modifications to the
LGPL-covered component, and must provide the corresponding source and
relinking information above.
